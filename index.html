Frequently asked questions

How do I sync my progress to another device?
Sign in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on on both devices. Progress syncs automatically; it can take a minute.

Pronunciation practice doesn't work.
Allow microphone and speech recognition access in the iOS Settings app under Learn German. In a quiet place the check works best.

I can't hear the German audio.
Turn off silent mode and check the volume. For a better voice, add a German voice in Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices.

Can I switch the explanations between English and Chinese?
Yes, in the app's My tab.

How do I start again?
Use the reset option in the app's settings.
